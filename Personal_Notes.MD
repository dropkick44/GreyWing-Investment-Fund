Personal Notes Regarding the Project:

*What GreyWings Investment Management Fund will be:*

- The project is a web application that allows users to manage their investments and research.
- The project is built with React, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS.
- The project is hosted on Vercel, with Supabase for the database and authentication.
- This platform will be a centralised platform where all of our tools, research, methodologies, etc. will be housed.
- This platform will be used by the investment team to manage the fund(s) and the clients.
- This platform will be used by the research team to manage the research and development of new strategies.
- This platform will be used by the operations team to manage the day to day operations of the fund(s).

- This platform will ultimately be what makes us profitable or not.

*What GreyWings Investment Management Fund software will not be:*

- The project will not be a trading platform.
- The project will not be a broker.
- The project will not be a custodian.
- The project will not be a payment processor.




- The project is a dashboard for a trading firm to manage their investments and research.
- The project will have a heavy focus on large data inflows and visualizations.
- The project will have a focus on real-time data and streaming data.
