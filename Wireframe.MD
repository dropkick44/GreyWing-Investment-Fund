Here’s a sample UI wireframe/mockup for your dashboard, broken down into key sections:

---

## 1. Internal Dashboard (Proprietary Trading & Research)

### Header
- **Logo:** Your company logo
- **Navigation:** Home, Portfolio, Research, Alerts, Reports, Settings
- **User Profile:** Logout, Notifications, User Settings

### Main Dashboard

#### Left Sidebar
- **Quick Actions:** New Trade, New Research, Alerts
- **Favorites:** Pinned assets, research notes, alerts

#### Center (Main Content)

- **Portfolio Overview**
  - **Summary Card:** Total P&L, Net Worth, Daily Change
  - **Holdings Table:** Asset, Quantity, Entry Price, Current Price, P&L, Allocation
  - **Allocation Chart:** Pie chart showing asset allocation

- **Live Market Data**
  - **Price Chart:** Interactive time series chart (1D, 1W, 1M, 1Y)
  - **Order Book:** Bid/Ask spread, volume
  - **Market News:** Latest headlines, sentiment indicators

- **Trade & Position Tracker**
  - **Open Positions:** Asset, Entry Price, Current Price, P&L, Stop Loss, Take Profit
  - **Trade History:** Past trades, performance metrics

- **Research & Analytics**
  - **Research Notes:** Custom notes, predictions, signals
  - **Analytics:** Backtesting results, performance metrics

- **Alerts & Notifications**
  - **Active Alerts:** Price triggers, news alerts, risk warnings
  - **Notification Center:** Inbox for alerts and updates

#### Right Sidebar
- **Market Sentiment:** Social sentiment, economic calendar
- **Whale Activity:** Large trades, unusual flows
- **Quick Stats:** Key metrics, market indices

---

## 2. Investor/Customer Dashboard

### Header
- **Logo:** Your company logo
- **Navigation:** Home, My Investments, Performance, News, Support
- **User Profile:** Logout, Notifications, Settings

### Main Dashboard

#### Left Sidebar
- **Quick Actions:** View Reports, Contact Support
- **Favorites:** Pinned investments, news

#### Center (Main Content)

- **Investment Overview**
  - **Summary Card:** Total Investment, Current Value, Performance
  - **Holdings Table:** Asset, Quantity, Current Value, Performance
  - **Performance Chart:** Time series chart of investment growth

- **News & Updates**
  - **Latest News:** Headlines relevant to your investments
  - **Market Sentiment:** Sentiment indicators

- **Reports & Documents**
  - **Download Reports:** PDF, Excel reports
  - **Documents:** Legal documents, statements

#### Right Sidebar
- **Support:** Contact information, FAQs
- **Quick Stats:** Key metrics, market indices

---

## 3. Mobile View (Responsive Design)

- **Collapsible Sidebar:** Hamburger menu for navigation
- **Cards Layout:** Stacked cards for portfolio, market data, news
- **Touch-Friendly:** Larger buttons, swipe gestures

---

## 4. Design Elements

- **Color Scheme:** Professional, finance-focused (blues, greens, grays)
- **Typography:** Clean, readable fonts (e.g., Roboto, Inter)
- **Icons:** Material Design or Font Awesome
- **Charts:** Interactive, responsive (Plotly.js, Recharts)
- **Tables:** Sortable, filterable, paginated
